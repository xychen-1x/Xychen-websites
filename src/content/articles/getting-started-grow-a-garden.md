Grow a Garden is a Roblox farming experience built around planting seeds, waiting for crops to grow, and harvesting what you have planted. The official game description also notes that your garden continues growing while you are offline.

This guide covers only the basic steps described on the game's official Roblox page. It does not assume fixed shop timers, crop growth durations, or other values that the official description does not specify.

## 1. Get seeds

Visit the Seed Shop and look at the seeds currently available. The official game description says to buy seeds when the shop restocks. Stock can change, so check the in-game shop rather than relying on an old list.

## 2. Equip a seed

Choose the seed you want to plant and equip it. Make sure you are standing in your own garden before planting.

## 3. Plant it on a plot

Select one of the brown plots in your farm and click or tap it while the seed is equipped. The official instructions describe this as the basic planting action.

## 4. Wait for the crop to grow

Give the planted crop time to grow. The official description does not provide one universal growth timer, so this guide does not assign a specific duration.

## 5. Harvest the crop

When the crop is fully grown, harvest it. You can then continue tending your garden and planting other seeds.

## Does the garden grow while you are offline?

Yes. The official Roblox experience description explicitly says that the garden continues growing while you are offline. It does not specify an exact offline growth limit or a complete timing table, so avoid treating unofficial numbers as guaranteed.

## Keep in mind

- Check the in-game Seed Shop for its current stock.
- Follow the planting instructions shown in the game.
- Treat exact growth times and shop schedules as unverified unless a current, reliable source confirms them.
- Game systems can change after updates; this guide is limited to the basic instructions on the official experience page.
